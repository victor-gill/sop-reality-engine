MERIDIAN LOGISTICS — STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURES (Finance)
Effective: January 2026

STEP 1
Trigger: Monthly bookkeeping close
Action: Pay Ledgible bookkeeping subscription
Condition: Amount is $89/month, auto-pay if under $500
Role: Operations Manager

STEP 2
Trigger: New vendor invoice received
Action: Verify all vendor invoices against purchase orders
Condition: If amount > 5000, route to COO for approval before payment
Role: Accounts Payable Clerk

STEP 3
Trigger: Quarterly safety audit
Action: Pay SafeCert Auditing for facility safety certification
Condition: Amount is $1,200 per quarter, net-30 terms
Role: Compliance Lead

STEP 4
Trigger: Monthly telecom billing cycle
Action: Pay Twilio API usage invoice
Condition: Amount is approximately $120/month, auto-pay enabled
Role: Tech Lead

STEP 5
Trigger: Bi-weekly contractor payroll run
Action: Pay Northbridge Staffing for contracted drivers
Condition: Amount varies by headcount, approximately $4,200 bi-weekly
Role: Operations Manager

STEP 6
Trigger: Monthly fuel card reconciliation
Action: Pay RouteFuel fleet fuel card balance
Condition: Amount is approximately $800/month, due on the 10th
Role: Fleet Coordinator
